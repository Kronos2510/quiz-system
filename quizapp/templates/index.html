Clear boundaries

Three roles. No accidental access.

H

Hosts

  • Request a host account, approved by the platform admin
  • Create, publish, close and reopen quizzes
  • Approve participants and rotate access codes
  • Grade written answers and release results
P

Participants

  • Register and wait for a host to approve the account
  • Join only with an access code from their host
  • Take timed attempts with automatic saving
  • See scores and feedback after the host releases them
A

Admin

  • Approve or reject host applications
  • Reset passwords and disable accounts
  • Keep an audit trail of approvals and releases
  • Step in when a participant needs a fallback approval
